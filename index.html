How to Play

  1. Inspect. Tap equipment, then tap every instrument to read it. No hissing, no flicker — waste only shows up in the data.
  2. Diagnose. Pick the right action. Wrong = −30 s and the finding is lost. Spot efficient gear with “Already efficient” for +15 s.
  3. Quantify. Pick the annual $ savings. Right = full $, wrong = half. Most $/yr in 6:00 wins.

Defaults: 6,000 h/yr · $0.08/kWh · $12/kW-month · $8/MMBtu
